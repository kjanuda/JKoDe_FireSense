Subject: Request for PMMA microgravity flame-spread data for independent validation research

Dear Dr. Meyer,

I am developing FireSense, a research decision-support system for
evidence-grounded analysis of reduced-gravity flame-spread behaviour.

I am currently looking for an independent experimental dataset to validate
a PMMA thin-sheet flame-spread baseline.

The specific condition of interest is:

- PMMA thin sheet
- approximately 0.2 mm / 200 µm thickness
- 21 vol.% oxygen
- approximately 101.3 kPa
- true microgravity
- 50 mm/s opposed flow

I have reviewed the work reported in:

Ries, Eigenbrod and Meyer,
"Effect of oxygen concentration, pressure, and opposed flow velocity on
the flame spread along thin PMMA sheets",
Proceedings of the Combustion Institute, 2024,
DOI: 10.1016/j.proci.2024.105358.

I would be very grateful if you could clarify whether your Bremen drop-tower
campaign contains runs matching, or closely approaching, the condition above.

If available, I am particularly interested in run-level numerical data such as:

- specimen thickness
- oxygen concentration
- pressure
- opposed-flow velocity
- flame-spread rate
- run/experiment identifier
- uncertainty or within-run variability

Three or more independent runs at the same condition would be especially
valuable.

The data would be used strictly as holdout validation evidence and would not
be used for model training or refitting. Source provenance and uncertainty
information would be preserved explicitly.

If the exact 50 mm/s condition was not tested, information about the nearest
available conditions would also be useful as independent comparison evidence.

Thank you very much for your time and consideration.

Best regards,
